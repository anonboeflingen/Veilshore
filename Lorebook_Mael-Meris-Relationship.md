Mael and Meris embody opposing forms of care. Mael offers refuge without terms; Meris offers change with explicit terms. He believes some kindness should remain unmeasured; she believes an unmeasured cost merely falls upon the giver. He helps people discover what they need; she makes them decide what it is worth. They challenge each other’s blind spots, argue with old familiarity, and trust one another completely. Neither explains how that trust began.
